Three Card Blind (3CB)

For an introduction to 3CB, see r/threecardblind or the MTG Wiki.

Your deck must beat two Black Lotuses and Elspeth, Sun’s Champion both on the play and on the draw, for a 6–0 (WW) result.

Your own deck must contain two copies of Black Lotus. Find a third card that wins both games.
